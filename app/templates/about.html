About This Project

Multi-Class Eye Disease Prediction Using EfficientNetB4 and ODIR-5K is an M.Tech research project that applies transfer learning to automatically detect eight ocular conditions from fundus retinal photographs.

Author: Aiyesha Rukhsar Institute: NIT Delhi Model: EfficientNetB4 (ImageNet → ODIR fine-tuned) Dataset: ODIR-5K Framework: TensorFlow / Keras
